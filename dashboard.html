    Reproduce a chapter

    1. In the Chapters table, press Details on the chapter's row, then Download this package.
    2. Extract the zip file into a folder of its own. Running it from inside the zip will not work.
    3. Double-click the .Rproj file in the extracted folder, so that RStudio opens in the right place.
    4. The first time, open install_packages.R and press Source.
    5. Open run_all.R and press Source. Your results appear in the output folder.
    6. Compare output/results.txt and output/figures.pdf with the chapter.
    source("run_all.R")
